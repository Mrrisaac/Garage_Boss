# Remaster Status

## Benchmark 1: Garage Division Headquarters

**Status:** Playable vertical slice

Implemented:

- 320×200 pixel canvas with crisp nearest-neighbor scaling
- Original Garage Division Headquarters pixel-art room
- Richie pathfinding, walking animation, and depth sorting
- Christine, phone, television, fluorescent-light, and office-plant animation
- 17 clickable hotspots with repeat-click narrator responses
- WALK, LOOK, TALK, TAKE, USE, and TYPE controls
- Complete Vanishing Key Ring puzzle
- Complete Phantom Monthly Payment puzzle
- Inventory, achievements, score, stress, time, and case tracking
- Manual save slot, autosave, hints, synthesized sound, music, and fullscreen
- Comedic game-over sequence with rewind
- Responsive desktop and Android presentation

Preview: `https://mrrisaac.github.io/Garage_Boss/remaster/`

The Classic Edition remains untouched at the repository root. The `classic-live` branch remains the archival snapshot, and all remaster engine work stays on the `remaster` branch until release approval.
